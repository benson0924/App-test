\chapter{Linear Algebra for Quantum Computing}

Quantum states are vectors; quantum gates are matrices. This chapter introduces the Dirac notation and linear tools used throughout the book.

\section{Kets and Bras}

We write $\ket{\psi}=\begin{bmatrix}\alpha\\\beta\end{bmatrix}$ and $\bra{\psi}=\begin{bmatrix}\alpha^*&\beta^*\end{bmatrix}$.

\section{Inner Products}

For $\ket{\phi}=\begin{bmatrix}a\\b\end{bmatrix}$ and $\ket{\psi}=\begin{bmatrix}c\\d\end{bmatrix}$,
\[
\braket{\phi|\psi}=a^*c+b^*d,\qquad \|\ket{\psi}\|=\sqrt{\braket{\psi|\psi}}.
\]
Computational basis vectors are orthonormal: $\braket{0|0}=\braket{1|1}=1$ and $\braket{0|1}=0$.

\section{Projection and Measurement}

The amplitude for outcome $\ket{m}$ is $\braket{m|\psi}$ and
\[
P(m)=|\braket{m|\psi}|^2.
\]
The projector is $P_m=\ket{m}\bra{m}$.

\section{Outer Products and Completeness}

$\ket{\psi}\bra{\phi}$ is a rank-one matrix. Completeness in the computational basis:
\[
\ket{0}\bra{0}+\ket{1}\bra{1}=I.
\]

\section{Unitary Matrices}

A gate $U$ is unitary if $U^\dagger U=UU^\dagger=I$, hence $U^{-1}=U^\dagger$. Unitary evolution preserves normalization:
\[
\braket{\psi'|\psi'}=\braket{\psi|U^\dagger U|\psi}=\braket{\psi|\psi}.
\]
