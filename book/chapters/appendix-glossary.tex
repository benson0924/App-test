\begin{description}
\item[Amplitude] Complex coefficient of a basis state.
\item[Ancilla] Auxiliary qubit used during a computation.
\item[Basis] Orthonormal set spanning a Hilbert space.
\item[Bit] Classical two-level information unit.
\item[Bloch sphere] Geometric picture of a pure qubit state.
\item[BQP] Bounded-error quantum polynomial time complexity class.
\item[CNOT] Controlled-NOT gate.
\item[Decoherence] Loss of quantum coherence via environment coupling.
\item[Entanglement] Non-separable quantum correlation.
\item[Gate] Reversible operation on bits or qubits.
\item[Global phase] Overall phase with no observable effect.
\item[Hadamard] Gate mapping Z and X bases.
\item[Hilbert space] Complex vector space of quantum states.
\item[Ket] Dirac vector notation $\ket{\psi}$.
\item[Measurement] Probabilistic extraction of classical outcomes.
\item[Oracle] Black-box function access in algorithms.
\item[Phase] Argument of a complex amplitude.
\item[Qubit] Two-level quantum information unit.
\item[Superposition] Linear combination of basis states.
\item[Tensor product] Composition of subsystem state spaces.
\item[Unitary] Norm-preserving reversible linear operator.
\end{description}
